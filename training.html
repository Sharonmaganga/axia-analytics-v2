Team AI training

AI training for every role in your team.

Practical programmes for executives, marketers, accountants, customer service and sales, built around Microsoft Copilot and the tools your team already uses, with clear rules for keeping member data safe.

By Sharon Maganga, AI Corporate Trainer · Axia Analytics

How every programme works

Step 1A 5-lesson foundation on safe AI use: what may be pasted, where, and who checks the answer.
Step 2Your role track: hands-on lessons of about 25 minutes, using your team's own tasks.
Step 3A capstone project, with the time saved measured before and after.
Step 4An Axia Certificate of Completion for each person who finishes.

The certificate is issued by Axia Analytics. It is not a Microsoft certification or an accredited qualification.

Keeping your data safe

Every team learns one simple rule before touching real work, and takes home a one-page safe-use card and a template AI use policy for the organisation.

Green: public or genericFine in any approved tool.
Amber: internal, anonymisedApproved tool only, with names, IDs and account numbers removed first.
Red: personal and confidentialNever entered into an AI tool.

Every organisation confirms its own rules with its legal adviser or data-protection officer. Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 applies to personal data.
