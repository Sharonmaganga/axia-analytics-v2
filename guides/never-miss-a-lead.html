Never miss a lead: AI follow-up with Zapier or Make

Capture every enquiry in one place and draft a quick reply automatically, so no lead waits for days.

Zapier or Make, Google Sheets, Gmail60 to 90 minutesYou will measure: Lead reply time and reply rate

By Sharon Maganga, AI Corporate Trainer · Axia Analytics

Who this is for

Small teams that get enquiries from a form, email or social media and respond late or not at all.

What you need

  • A lead source such as a Google Form or website form
  • A free Zapier or Make account (free plans have limits)
  • Gmail and Google Sheets

The steps

  1. Choose one sourceStart with a single form. Add other sources after the first one works.
  2. Build the triggerIn Zapier or Make, create an automation that starts when a new form response arrives.
  3. Log the leadAdd a step that writes the lead into a Google Sheet with the time it arrived.
  4. Draft the reply with AIAdd an AI step using the prompt below and save the result as a Gmail draft. Do not auto-send until you trust it.
  5. Alert yourselfAdd a step that sends you an email or message with the lead's details and the draft.
  6. Add a reminderIf the lead has not replied in two days, create a reminder to follow up.

Copy-and-paste prompt

Write a short, friendly reply to this enquiry from [name] at [organisation]: [enquiry text].
Thank them, restate what they asked for in one line, propose two times for a 20-minute call, and keep it under 90 words. Do not promise prices or dates.

Replace everything in [square brackets] with your own details.

Measure your result

Run it on five real tasks. Write down the number before you start and again after. The number is the point.

Tips and cautions

  • Keep a human in the loop for the first month.
  • Automations that touch customer data need the same care as any other system.
