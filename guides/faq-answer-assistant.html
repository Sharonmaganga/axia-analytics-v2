Turn your FAQs into a 24/7 answer assistant, no code

Collect your most repeated questions, write approved answers once, and let an assistant answer them for your team.

ChatGPT Project or Gemini Gem45 minutesYou will measure: Hours a week answering repeat questions

By Sharon Maganga, AI Corporate Trainer · Axia Analytics

Who this is for

Teams that answer the same questions every day by phone, email or WhatsApp, such as loan requirements, opening hours or statement requests.

What you need

  • Your 20 to 50 most repeated questions
  • Approved answers for each, including contacts and opening hours
  • A ChatGPT account (Projects) or a Google account (Gems)

The steps

  1. Find the repeat questionsScroll through the last month of WhatsApp, email and call notes. Note every question you answered more than twice.
  2. Write one approved answer for eachPut them in a single document: question, answer, who to contact if the person needs more. Keep each answer short and checked by someone who knows the policy.
  3. Create the assistantCreate a Project or Gem and upload the document. Add the instructions below so it only answers from your document.
  4. Test with 20 real questionsAsk them in different wording. Mark each answer right, wrong or incomplete, then fix the document, not the assistant.
  5. Give it to your team firstLet staff use it internally for two weeks. This is the safest way to find gaps before any member or customer sees it.
  6. Keep it currentEvery week, add the new questions people asked. When you want the same assistant to answer members directly on WhatsApp or your website, that is a build we can do for you.

Copy-and-paste prompt

You answer questions for [organisation name] using ONLY the attached FAQ document.
If the answer is not in the document, say: 'I do not have that information. Please contact [name / phone / email].'
Keep answers short and in plain language. Do not guess prices, dates or policies.
If a question involves a complaint, money or a personal account, tell the person to contact [name / phone].

Replace everything in [square brackets] with your own details.

Measure your result

Run it on five real tasks. Write down the number before you start and again after. The number is the point.

Tips and cautions

  • Before you paste real member, client or financial data into any AI tool, check your organisation's data policy. While testing, remove names, ID numbers and account numbers.
  • Fixing the FAQ document improves every future answer.
