Build your AI assistant that writes in your voice

Set up a reusable assistant that drafts emails and posts the way you write, so you stop starting from a blank page.

ChatGPT Project or Gemini Gem30 minutesYou will measure: Minutes to draft an email or post, before vs after

By Sharon Maganga, AI Corporate Trainer · Axia Analytics

Who this is for

Anyone who writes emails, posts or proposals every week and edits generic AI text to make it sound like them.

What you need

  • 5 samples of your best writing (emails, posts or messages)
  • A ChatGPT account (Projects) or a Google account (Gems)
  • 30 minutes

The steps

  1. Collect your samplesPick 5 pieces that sound like you at your best. Remove client names and private details.
  2. Ask the AI to describe your voicePaste the samples and ask it to describe your sentence length, tone, favourite phrases and words you never use. Read the answer and correct anything that is wrong.
  3. Create the assistantIn ChatGPT, create a new Project and add instructions and your samples as files. In Gemini, create a new Gem and paste the instructions. Menu names change, so look for Projects or Gems.
  4. Write clear instructionsUse the template below: who you are, who you write for, your voice rules, the format you want, and an instruction to ask you questions when details are missing.
  5. Test it on three real tasksDraft a real email, a real post and a real reply. Fix what sounds off, then add that fix to the instructions so it sticks.
  6. Use it daily and refine weeklyEvery Friday, add one new rule based on what you edited most that week.

Copy-and-paste prompt

You are my writing assistant. You write as me.
About me: [your role, your organisation, what you do].
My readers: [who you write to].
My voice: [for example: plain words, short sentences, warm but direct, no hype, no jargon].
Never use: [words and phrases you dislike].
Format: [for example: emails under 120 words; LinkedIn posts with a short hook line].
Before drafting, ask me up to 2 questions if key details are missing.
After drafting, list anything you assumed so I can check it.

Replace everything in [square brackets] with your own details.

Measure your result

Run it on five real tasks. Write down the number before you start and again after. The number is the point.

Tips and cautions

  • Add new good examples to the Project every month.
  • Keep the assistant for drafting. You still read and approve everything before it goes out.
