Qualified but invisible? Fix your LinkedIn profile in 5 steps

Jobs are 90% connection. The other 10% has to be findable, and it lives on your LinkedIn profile. Here are the five fixes recruiters notice, with the ChatGPT prompts to do each one in minutes.

By Sharon Maganga, Axia Analytics

About the samples: the example headlines and lines on this page are written for a data analyst. Yours should come from your own CV. Paste the prompts into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI chat.

01Photo

Professional, and still you

Your photo sits next to every comment, message and application. A blank circle or a cropped group photo makes people scroll past.

  • Recent, with your face filling most of the frame.
  • Good light, a plain background, and you looking at the camera.
  • No sunglasses, no heavy filters.

No good photo? Upload a clear selfie to ChatGPT and type /linkedinheadshot. Want more control? Add the line below, then check the result still looks like you.

Add to your request
Keep my face exactly as it is, grey background, navy blazer.
02Banner

One sentence: what you do

The banner is the wide image behind your photo. The default blue wastes the biggest space on your profile.

  • Use a free Canva LinkedIn banner template, sized 1584 × 396 px.
  • Write one sentence that says what you do.
  • Keep the bottom-left empty, because your photo covers it.
Sample · use your own

Data analyst turning business data into decisions

03Headline

Role + skills + value

Your headline shows in search results, under your name on every comment, and in every connection request. Build it in three parts:

  1. Target role: Data Analyst
  2. Core skills from job ads: SQL, Python & Power BI
  3. The value you bring: Turning business data into actionable insights
ChatGPT prompt
I'm targeting [job title] roles in [industry]. Here's my CV: [paste]. Write 5 LinkedIn headlines under 220 characters using this formula: target role, 3 core skills from job ads for this role, and the value I bring. Only use skills that appear in my CV.
Sample · use your own CV

Data Analyst | SQL, Python & Power BI | Turning business data into actionable insights

04About

Not a school composition

Skip the words every profile uses:

hardworkingpassionateresults-driven

Write it in four parts instead:

  1. Who you are, in one line.
  2. What you do, and for whom.
  3. Proof: 2–3 real results.
  4. The role you want next, plus your email.
ChatGPT prompt
Write my LinkedIn About section in first person, under 1,500 characters, in those four parts. Warm, plain language. No words like passionate, hardworking or results-driven. Don't invent any numbers. My CV: [paste].

If you use this prompt in a new chat, paste the four parts above in with it.

05Skills + proof

Make it easy to say yes

Recruiters filter candidates by skill. If a skill isn't on your profile, you may not show up in their search.

  • Open 3 job ads for the role you want and copy the skills they share.
  • Add the ones you can prove, and pin your top 3.

02 / Bonus

Two more things worth five minutes

Ask for recommendations

Ask a former manager and a colleague. A short recommendation is proof in someone else's words.

Message template
Hi [name], I hope you're well. I'm updating my LinkedIn profile as I look for [type of role]. Would you be willing to write me a short recommendation about our work on [project or role]? Thank you!

Fill in your contact info

A recruiter who likes your profile needs a way to reach you.

  • Add a professional email address.
  • Turn on Open to Work.
  • Name the role you want next in your About section.
