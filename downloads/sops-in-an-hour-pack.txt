WRITE SOPS AND TRAINING GUIDES IN AN HOUR
============================================================
Tools: Any AI chat tool plus a screen or voice recording
Time: 60 minutes per SOP
You will measure: Hours per SOP and days to onboard a new person

WHAT YOU NEED
  - One process you repeat often
  - A way to record your voice or screen
  - An AI chat tool

CHECKLIST
  [ ] 1. Pick one process: Choose something you do weekly, such as processing a request, closing the month or onboarding a customer.
  [ ] 2. Record yourself doing it: Narrate each step out loud, including what you check and what can go wrong.
  [ ] 3. Transcribe it: Use captions or an AI transcription tool to get the text.
  [ ] 4. Turn it into an SOP: Use the prompt below. Review the result for missing steps and wrong order.
  [ ] 5. Add screenshots and checks: Add images for the tricky steps and a short checklist at the end.
  [ ] 6. Test it with a new person: Ask someone unfamiliar to follow it without your help. Fix every place they get stuck.

COPY-AND-PASTE PROMPT
------------------------------------------------------------
Turn this transcript into a standard operating procedure with: Purpose, Who does it, Tools needed, Numbered steps, Checks before finishing, Common mistakes, and What to do if something goes wrong.
Use short sentences and plain language. If a step is unclear, list it under 'Questions to confirm' instead of guessing.
