THE MEETING-TO-ACTION SYSTEM
============================================================
Tools: Copilot in Teams or Gemini in Meet
Time: 20 minutes to set up
You will measure: Follow-up time per meeting

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Microsoft Teams with Copilot, or Google Meet with Gemini notes (availability depends on your plan and your admin's settings)
  - Permission to record or transcribe meetings
  - A simple place to track actions (a sheet, Planner or Tasks)

CHECKLIST
  [ ] 1. Check what your plan includes: Ask your IT admin whether meeting transcription and AI recap are switched on for you. If not, you can still paste a transcript or your own notes into a general AI tool.
  [ ] 2. Switch on notes before the meeting: Start the transcript or notes feature at the start and tell attendees. Never record without telling people.
  [ ] 3. Ask for the five things that matter: Right after the meeting, use the prompt below to get decisions, actions, owners, deadlines and open questions.
  [ ] 4. Send the follow-up within 15 minutes: Paste the result into a short email: what was decided, who does what by when. Read it once before you send.
  [ ] 5. Log the actions: Copy the actions into your tracker so they live somewhere other than your inbox.
  [ ] 6. Review weekly: Once a week, paste your action list and ask the AI which items are overdue or have no owner.

COPY-AND-PASTE PROMPT
------------------------------------------------------------
From this meeting transcript or notes, produce:
1. Decisions made (one line each).
2. Actions: what, owner, due date (write 'no date agreed' if none).
3. Open questions that need an answer.
4. A 5-line follow-up email I can send to all attendees.
Use only what is in the text. If something is unclear, say so instead of guessing.

  - Say owners and dates out loud in the meeting. The AI can only capture what was said.
