THE PROPOSAL GENERATOR: FROM CALL NOTES TO PROPOSAL IN 20 MINUTES
============================================================
Tools: Any AI chat tool plus your proposal template
Time: 20 minutes per proposal
You will measure: Proposal turnaround time and win rate

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Your best past proposal with client details removed
  - Notes from the discovery call
  - An AI chat tool

CHECKLIST
  [ ] 1. Build your template: Keep the structure: Problem, Approach, Timeline, Investment, Terms. Remove client details.
  [ ] 2. Take structured notes on calls: Capture the client's problem, goal, deadline, budget range and decision maker.
  [ ] 3. Draft with AI: Give the AI your template and your notes using the prompt below.
  [ ] 4. Fill the numbers yourself: You set prices, dates and terms. Never let the AI decide these.
  [ ] 5. Edit for fit: Remove generic lines. Add one sentence showing you listened, using the client's own words.
  [ ] 6. Send it the same day: Export to PDF and send within the hour. Record the outcome so you can calculate your win rate.

------------------------------------------------------------
Using the proposal template below and my call notes, write a first draft proposal.
Keep my template's headings. Use the client's own words for the problem.
Leave prices, dates and legal terms as [TO CONFIRM]. Do not invent facts about the client.
