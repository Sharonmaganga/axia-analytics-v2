NEVER MISS A LEAD: AI FOLLOW-UP WITH ZAPIER OR MAKE
============================================================
Tools: Zapier or Make, Google Sheets, Gmail
Time: 60 to 90 minutes
You will measure: Lead reply time and reply rate

WHAT YOU NEED
  - A lead source such as a Google Form or website form
  - A free Zapier or Make account (free plans have limits)
  - Gmail and Google Sheets

CHECKLIST
  [ ] 1. Choose one source: Start with a single form. Add other sources after the first one works.
  [ ] 2. Build the trigger: In Zapier or Make, create an automation that starts when a new form response arrives.
  [ ] 3. Log the lead: Add a step that writes the lead into a Google Sheet with the time it arrived.
  [ ] 4. Draft the reply with AI: Add an AI step using the prompt below and save the result as a Gmail draft. Do not auto-send until you trust it.
  [ ] 5. Alert yourself: Add a step that sends you an email or message with the lead's details and the draft.
  [ ] 6. Add a reminder: If the lead has not replied in two days, create a reminder to follow up.

COPY-AND-PASTE PROMPT
------------------------------------------------------------
Write a short, friendly reply to this enquiry from [name] at [organisation]: [enquiry text].
Thank them, restate what they asked for in one line, propose two times for a 20-minute call, and keep it under 90 words. Do not promise prices or dates.
------------------------------------------------------------
