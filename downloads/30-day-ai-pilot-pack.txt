AXIA ANALYTICS - FREE GUIDE PACK 10
RUN A 30-DAY AI PILOT AND PROVE ROI TO YOUR BOSS
============================================================
Tools: Any of the tools in these guides
Time: 30 days, about 15 minutes a week to manage
You will measure: Hours saved per person per week

WHAT YOU NEED
  - One workflow and 3 to 5 volunteers
  - A baseline measurement for that workflow
  - A simple tracker for hours saved

CHECKLIST
  [ ] 1. Choose one workflow: Pick a repeated task with clear before-and-after time, such as a weekly report or meeting follow-ups.
  [ ] 2. Measure the baseline: For one week, record how long the task takes and how many times it happens.
  [ ] 3. Run a 15-minute kickoff: Show the volunteers the guide and the prompts. Agree what they will record.
  [ ] 4. Check in weekly: Spend 15 minutes each week on what worked, what failed and the hours saved.
  [ ] 5. Calculate the result: Use the ROI formula below. Count only time that was actually saved.
  [ ] 6. Present one page: Result, cost, risks and a clear ask: stop, extend, or scale. Use the prompt below to draft it.

COPY-AND-PASTE PROMPT
------------------------------------------------------------
Write a one-page summary for [boss's name] of a 30-day AI pilot.
Data: workflow [name]; [number] people; baseline [hours per person per week]; after pilot [hours per person per week]; tool cost [amount]; issues found [list].
Include: result, estimated annual value, risks and how we managed them, and a recommendation (stop, extend or scale). Use only the numbers I gave you.
------------------------------------------------------------

MEASURE YOUR RESULT (run it on 5 real tasks, write the numbers down)
  Measure                                           Before      After       
  Baseline hours per person per week                ______      ______      
  Hours per person per week after 30 days           ______      ______      
  Number of people in the pilot                     ______      ______      

ROI estimate = (hours saved per person per week x number of people x hourly cost x 4 weeks) minus tool and set-up cost. Use real figures only.

TIPS
  - A small, honest result beats a big, unproven one.
  - Report problems too. It builds trust in your numbers.
