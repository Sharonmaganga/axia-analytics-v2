AXIA ANALYTICS - FREE GUIDE PACK 05
ONE VIDEO INTO TEN POSTS: THE REPURPOSING MACHINE
============================================================
Tools: Any AI chat tool plus your video or voice note
Time: 60 minutes
You will measure: Posts produced per hour

WHAT YOU NEED
  - One 5 to 15 minute video or voice note on a single topic
  - A transcript (auto captions or an AI transcription tool)
  - An AI chat tool; your voice assistant from Guide 1 if you built it

CHECKLIST
  [ ] 1. Record one useful talk: Pick one topic you know well and explain it as if to a client. Aim for 5 to 15 minutes.
  [ ] 2. Get the transcript: Use your video platform's captions or an AI transcription tool. Fix names and numbers.
  [ ] 3. Pull out ten distinct points: Use the prompt below. Each point should stand alone.
  [ ] 4. Choose a format for each: Mix short text posts, a carousel outline, a question, a story and a quote.
  [ ] 5. Edit in your voice: Rewrite the weak lines. Remove anything you would not say yourself.
  [ ] 6. Schedule across two weeks: Spread posts out and reuse the best-performing one again next month.

COPY-AND-PASTE PROMPT
------------------------------------------------------------
Here is a transcript of me explaining [topic].
Extract 10 distinct, useful points a [audience] could act on. For each point write: a one-line hook, a 60-word post, and a suggested format (text, carousel, question, story or quote).
Keep my wording where possible. Do not add facts that are not in the transcript.
------------------------------------------------------------
