AXIA ANALYTICS - FREE GUIDE PACK 03
TURN YOUR FAQS INTO A 24/7 ANSWER ASSISTANT, NO CODE
============================================================
Tools: ChatGPT Project or Gemini Gem
Time: 45 minutes
You will measure: Hours a week answering repeat questions

WHAT YOU NEED
  - Your 20 to 50 most repeated questions
  - Approved answers for each, including contacts and opening hours
  - A ChatGPT account (Projects) or a Google account (Gems)

CHECKLIST
  [ ] 1. Find the repeat questions: Scroll through the last month of WhatsApp, email and call notes. Note every question you answered more than twice.
  [ ] 2. Write one approved answer for each: Put them in a single document: question, answer, who to contact if the person needs more. Keep each answer short and checked by someone who knows the policy.
  [ ] 3. Create the assistant: Create a Project or Gem and upload the document. Add the instructions below so it only answers from your document.
  [ ] 4. Test with 20 real questions: Ask them in different wording. Mark each answer right, wrong or incomplete, then fix the document, not the assistant.
  [ ] 5. Give it to your team first: Let staff use it internally for two weeks. This is the safest way to find gaps before any member or customer sees it.
  [ ] 6. Keep it current: Every week, add the new questions people asked. When you want the same assistant to answer members directly on WhatsApp or your website, that is a build we can do for you.

COPY-AND-PASTE PROMPT
------------------------------------------------------------
You answer questions for [organisation name] using ONLY the attached FAQ document.
If the answer is not in the document, say: 'I do not have that information. Please contact [name / phone / email].'
Keep answers short and in plain language. Do not guess prices, dates or policies.
If a question involves a complaint, money or a personal account, tell the person to contact [name / phone].
------------------------------------------------------------

TIPS
  - Before you paste real member, client or financial data into any AI tool, check your organisation's data policy. While testing, remove names, ID numbers and account numbers.
  - Fixing the FAQ document improves every future answer.
