The toolkit

Seven tools, one set of member data.

Each tool works on its own and reads the same exports, so a SACCO can start with the one that hurts most and add the rest later.

01

Contributions Reconciliation Engine

Matches check-off schedules, M-Pesa paybill, bank and cheque receipts against what every member should have paid.

  • Flags unremitted, under- and over-deductions
  • Finds owners of deposits with no reference
  • Member statements and a masked unidentified-deposits list
02

Loan Portfolio Monitor

Ages the loan book every month and shows portfolio at risk and the provisions it needs.

  • PAR at 1, 30 and 90 days
  • Guarantor exposure and exited guarantors
  • Deposit-multiple and concentration breaches
03

Board Pack Generator

Turns the trial balance into a board-ready pack with ratios against targets and a plain-English commentary.

  • Statement of financial position and income
  • Red flags written as sentences
  • 12-month trend
04

Loan Appraisal Assistant

Consistent, documented appraisal for salaried, self-employed and group borrowers.

  • Deposit multiple and repayment capacity
  • Guarantor free-capacity check
  • Largest amount that passes every rule
05

Dividend & Rebate Modeller

Tests year-end payout proposals against surplus, liquidity and capital before they reach the AGM.

  • Three scenarios side by side
  • Highest affordable rebate rate
  • What a typical member receives
06

WhatsApp Member Assistant

Balances, statements, loan estimates and payment help on WhatsApp, with verification first.

  • Read-only access to member data
  • Payment claims routed to finance
  • Hands over to staff when unsure
07

Data Protection Compliance Kit

A working register of duties under Kenya's Data Protection Act for financial cooperatives.

  • Obligations, data inventory and consent register
  • Member requests and breach log with deadlines
  • One-page dashboard for the board
Unidentified deposits

How a payment with no reference finds its owner.

Every receipt is tried against four checks, strongest first. Only the first two post automatically; the others are suggestions a staff member confirms, because a wrong posting is worse than a short delay.

CheckWhat it comparesWhat happens
1. Account referenceReference typed by the payer against member numbers, ignoring case and spacesPosted automatically
2. Phone numberPayer phone against the member register, 07… and 2547… treated as the samePosted automatically
3. Payer nameName on the payment against member namesSuggested; staff confirm
4. Amount patternAmount equal to exactly one direct-paying member's monthly dueSuggested; staff confirm
No matchNothing foundPublished on a masked list so the member can claim it
From spreadsheet to self-running

Start in Excel. Automate when you are ready.

Every tool starts as a workbook your team can use the same week. When the numbers match your current process, the same logic runs on a schedule inside your Microsoft 365, with a named approver before anything reaches members.

Option A uses scheduled exports from your core system; Option B connects by API. Nothing reaches a member without approval.
