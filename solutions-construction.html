The AI team

Six AI employees, one job each.

Each one owns a stage of the work, reports to a named person, and writes everything it does into one job record.

01

Front desk (voice and WhatsApp)

Answers calls and chats around the clock, captures the job details, explains the site-visit fee and books visits.

02

Estimator

Turns site-visit measurements into a draft quotation at your own rates, for a founder to approve.

03

Sales follow-up

Checks in on every open quote on schedule and flags the clients ready to go ahead.

04

Site coordinator

Collects evening progress, photos and material needs from supervisors and sends clients a weekly update.

05

Collections

Raises milestone invoices and sends polite, timely reminders before and after each due date.

06

Operations manager

Watches every job and tells the founders each morning what is stuck and who is waiting.

How they work together

No one forwards anything. The job record moves the work.

Every stage reads and writes the same job record, so the next AI employee picks up where the last one stopped.
