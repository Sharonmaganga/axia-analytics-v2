Track 2: agents

Order to ERP, without retyping.

Orders arriving by email or WhatsApp are read, matched line by line to the product master, and created in the ERP. Anything unclear goes back to the customer as one short question instead of a guess.

Disputed lines are emailed back for clarification; the clear lines wait with the order until the customer replies.

Customer onboarding with approvals built in.

A new account application collects documents once, checks them automatically, routes through each approver in Teams, matches the payment and tells the customer when their account or card is ready.

People approve; agents prepare, chase and record. Every step is logged for audit.
